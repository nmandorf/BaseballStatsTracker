## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Shared Live Game Score Card

The app SHALL use the same compact game-situation score card at the top of Offense and Defense.

#### Scenario: Switch live game mode

- **WHEN** the analyst switches between Offense and Defense
- **THEN** the game-mode controls and score card keep the same responsive gutter, spacing, visual hierarchy, and field ordering
- **AND** the score card shows inning half, inning number, current team phase, outs, team score, and opponent score
- **AND** mode-specific entry controls remain below the shared score card
